# Neighborhood Governance Council

## Type

Community Governance Body

## Mission

To enable residents to participate meaningfully in decisions affecting their neighborhood through innovative, inclusive governance practices.

## Description

The Neighborhood Governance Council brings together residents, local businesses, and community organizations to make decisions about neighborhood priorities, resources, and policies. Established in 2022, the Council has pioneered the use of consent-based decision making at the neighborhood level.

## Governance Structure

- Uses sociocratic principles with nested circles
- General Council (monthly meetings)
- Working Groups: Safety, Environment, Economic Development, Community Spaces
- All policy decisions made by consent

## Key Activities

- Monthly community assemblies
- Annual participatory budgeting process ($50,000)
- Neighborhood improvement projects
- Youth governance training program

## Members

- 12 elected council members
- 4 appointed liaisons (city, school, business, nonprofit)
- Open working group participation

## Impact

- 2023: Piloted consent-based decision making
- 2024: Launched participatory budgeting
- 2025: Expanded youth engagement program
- 2026: Documenting practices for replication

## Location

Operates in Riverside neighborhood, serves approximately 15,000 residents
