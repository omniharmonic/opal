# Engagement Flow

## Summary

A pattern for designing the journey of bringing new members into an organization or community, from initial contact through full participation.

## Problem

Organizations struggle to move people from awareness to active participation. Without intentional design, potential members fall through the cracks or don't understand how to get involved.

## Solution

Design an explicit flow that:
1. Maps the stages from initial contact to full engagement
2. Identifies what people need at each stage
3. Creates touchpoints and materials for each transition
4. Removes friction and clarifies next steps

## Context

Critical for:
- New organization launches
- Membership-based communities
- Volunteer coordination
- Movement building
