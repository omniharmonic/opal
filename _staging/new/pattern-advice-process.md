# Advice Process

## Summary

A decision-making pattern where individuals closest to a decision can make it autonomously after seeking advice from affected parties and those with relevant expertise.

## Problem

Traditional hierarchical decision-making is slow and doesn't leverage distributed expertise. Full consensus or consent processes can be too heavy for operational decisions.

## Solution

For operational decisions (not policy-level), the person closest to the decision:
1. Identifies who will be affected by the decision
2. Seeks advice from those affected and from domain experts
3. Makes the decision themselves, informed by the advice received

## Context

- Works well for operational decisions within established policies
- Often used alongside consent-based decision making for policy decisions
- Empowers individuals while maintaining collective input
