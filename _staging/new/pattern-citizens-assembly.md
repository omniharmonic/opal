# Citizens' Assembly

## Summary

A deliberative democracy pattern where randomly selected citizens are brought together to deliberate on complex issues, often used for constitutional reform or controversial policy decisions.

## Problem

Elected representatives may not reflect diverse perspectives, and complex issues benefit from extended deliberation that normal political processes don't allow.

## Solution

1. Random selection (sortition) of citizens, often stratified to mirror demographics
2. Information and learning phase with experts and materials
3. Facilitated deliberation over extended period
4. Recommendations produced for government/public

## Notable Examples

- **Ireland**: Used for constitutional reform (marriage equality, abortion)
- **France**: Citizens' Convention on Climate
- **Hundreds at local levels** worldwide

## Digital Support

- Learning modules and expert videos for preparation
- Real-time polling tools (Mentimeter) during sessions
- Communication of outcomes to broader public
