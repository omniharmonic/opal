# Double-Linking

## Summary

A governance pattern from sociocracy where circles (working groups) are connected by two representatives flowing in opposite directions, ensuring bidirectional communication and accountability.

## Problem

Hierarchical structures create information silos and power imbalances. Single representatives can become bottlenecks or fail to represent diverse perspectives.

## Solution

Between any two levels of organization:
1. One representative flows "upward" (elected by the lower circle to represent them in the higher circle)
2. One representative flows "downward" (appointed by the higher circle to coordinate with the lower)

Both representatives are full members of both circles they connect.

## Context

- Core component of sociocracy
- Scales well for organizations with hundreds of members
- Useful for connecting neighborhood work to city/regional level
