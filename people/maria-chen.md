# Maria Chen

## Role

Community Governance Facilitator and organizer working on democratic innovation at the neighborhood and municipal levels.

## Bio

Maria Chen has spent 15 years working at the intersection of community organizing and governance innovation. She currently facilitates the Neighborhood Governance Working Group and consults with municipal governments on implementing participatory processes.

## Expertise

- Facilitating consent-based decision making
- Training communities in sociocratic governance
- Designing participatory processes
- Building cross-sector coalitions

## Affiliations

- Neighborhood Governance Council (Facilitator)
- Municipal Innovation Lab (Advisor)
- National Civic Participation Network (Member)

## Contributions to Commons

- Led documentation of consent-based decision making patterns
- Contributed case studies on neighborhood governance
- Hosts monthly governance working group
